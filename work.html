OpePay — Digital Bank

Demo app · no real money · first load can take up to a minute

A fictional Nigerian mobile bank I designed and built end to end. Customers open an account, add demo funds, send money to other customers and to another bank, and get bank-style receipts. Every movement is recorded twice in a ledger so the books balance, and I tested 20 simultaneous payments to prove an account can never be overdrawn. Includes PIN-protected transfers, device verification, an audit trail, and a wrong-transfer dispute flow.

LuwaPay — Sister Bank & Payment Switch

Demo app · no real money · first load can take up to a minute

The sister bank to OpePay: a full banking app on its own, and together with OpePay it proves two independent banks can send money to each other safely. I built a payment switch where every message is digitally signed and time-stamped so it can't be forged or replayed, and timed-out transfers are checked before any refund so a customer is never charged twice. Own green-and-gold identity, database and server, sharing one codebase with OpePay.
